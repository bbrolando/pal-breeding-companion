Breeding Protocol

the rules that never change
SETUP
Farm & Cake

Unlock the Breeding Farm at Tech Lv19 and a Cooking Pot at Lv17. Drop one male + one female inside; place a Cake in the feed box or no egg forms.

How passives roll

STRATEGY
The 4-across split

Put your four target passives across the two parents — a 2/2, 1/3, or 0/4 split all work. Hatch repeatedly, keep the best child. Expect 6–10 eggs to converge on all four.

STAFFING
Specialists over generalists

1.0 raised work suitability to Lv10 and re-tuned every Pal. A Pal that does one job at Lv8 beats one that does five jobs at Lv2 — and it condenses far better. Assign one job per worker with the Monitoring Stand and keep fighters off the base.

05

Breeding for Passives

the ladder method, step by step

A hatchling's passives are drawn from the combined pool of both parents' passives (any duplicate is merged into one), and any slots left empty may then be filled with random passives from the whole library — including bad ones. Everything below is about controlling that pool and driving toward a full four-passive inherit. There are only ever 4 slots, and that cap never changes. The steps are universal; the ladder underneath adapts to whichever Pal you pick in the Breeding Bench dropdown up top.

1
Lock your 4 passives

Pick the exact final loadout from the Passive Library — four slots, no more. A mount wants Swift · Runner · Nimble · Legend; a worker wants Artisan · Serious · Lucky + one; a fighter wants Legend · Ferocious · Musclehead · Swift.

2
Gather clean sources

Catch wild Pals that already carry one target passive and little else. Wild Lucky Pals always have Lucky; Legendary Pals always have Legend. Fewer passives on a parent means better odds — every junk trait competes for a slot.

3
Keep the pool at four

Split your four targets across the two parents — 2/2, 1/3, or 0/4 all work. Don't let the combined pool exceed your four. Put your rarest passive on the male — the father's pass-down chance is slightly higher.

4
Ladder up

You rarely hit four in one hop. Breed toward a clean 2-passive child, save it, then pair it with a Pal holding the other two. Keep the best hatchling each generation as the next parent. "Wash" an unwanted trait by breeding with a 0-passive mate.

5
Drive to a full four

Once the two parents together hold exactly your four, hatch in bulk and keep only the child that inherits all four. A full four means zero empty slots, so no random junk can mutate in. That Pal is your template.

6
Mass-produce

Breed two Pals that each already hold the same four passives. The pool is only those four, so every hatchling draws exclusively from your set — and a big share roll the complete four. This stamps one perfect loadout across a whole team.

Legend is special

Legend never rolls randomly — it exists only on Legendary Pals. To put it on anything else, a parent or ancestor in the chain must be a Legendary carrying it.

Wild beats bred (Legendaries)

A bred Legendary has lower base stats than a wild-caught one unless it's an Alpha. For Jetragon, Frostallion and the like, catch the species first, then only same-species breed to fix passives.

Carry passives anywhere

Passives inherit independently of species. Build a donor with your four on any easy Pal, then follow the chart (the Bench, section 01) toward your target — the passives ride along.

Don't pollute the line

Passives can't be removed once on a Pal. Never breed junk traits into your chain, and wash stray ones with a 0-passive mate before continuing.
